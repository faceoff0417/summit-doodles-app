At Summit Doodles, we believe a company's statements and views around their mission, vision and values will be critical to their overall success. For some, a mission, vision and value statement just ends up being words on a sheet of paper, and at some point they can get off course from them. We strongly believe that our mission, vision and values have to start at the top, and must be adopted and lived every day! Our mission, vision and values are as follows:

Our Mission

Transform The Lives Of Families, One Puppy At A Time!

Our Vision

At Summit Doodles, our vision is to be the leading provider of exceptional and healthy puppies, enriching the lives of families and individuals through the joy and companionship that our puppies bring. We aspire to create a world where every puppy born in our care is welcomed into a loving home, cherished as a beloved family member, and enriches the lives of everyone it touches.

We are committed to upholding the highest standards of ethical breeding practices, prioritizing the health, well-being, and temperament of our puppies above all else. Through meticulous selection of breeding pairs, extensive health testing, and continuous improvement, we aim to consistently produce puppies of superior quality, both in appearance and temperament.

Our vision extends beyond breeding exceptional puppies. We strive to foster positive relationships with our clients, providing outstanding customer service, education, and support throughout the entire puppy adoption process and beyond. We believe in empowering our clients to become responsible and informed dog owners, promoting responsible pet ownership and the well-being of our puppies in their forever homes.

As a responsible member of the puppy breeding community, we are committed to contributing to the overall betterment of the canine world. We actively collaborate with veterinarians, trainers, and fellow breeders to advance the knowledge and understanding of responsible breeding practices, genetics, and puppy care.

We envision a future where our puppy breeding business serves as a role model for excellence, integrity, and compassion within the industry. Our commitment to transparency, honesty, and ethical conduct guides our every action, ensuring that we consistently exceed the expectations of our clients and make a positive impact on the lives of our puppies, their families, and the community at large.

Together, with our passionate team of dedicated professionals, we strive to create a legacy that exemplifies the very best in puppy breeding, inspiring trust, and delivering happiness one puppy at a time.

Our Core Values

At Summit Doodles, our core value is centered around the well-being and happiness of both our puppies and their future owners. We are committed to upholding the following principles:

1. Ethical Breeding Practices

We adhere to strict ethical standards and prioritize the health and welfare of our breeding dogs. We provide them with a safe and comfortable environment, regular veterinary care, proper nutrition, and ample exercise.

2. Health and Genetic Integrity

We prioritize the health and genetic integrity of our puppies by carefully selecting breeding pairs with sound health records and ensuring comprehensive health checks for each puppy before they go to their new homes. We aim to minimize the occurrence of hereditary disorders through responsible breeding practices.

3. Socialization and Mental Stimulation

We believe in the importance of early socialization and mental stimulation for our puppies. They are raised in a nurturing environment where they are exposed to various sights, sounds, and experiences to promote their development into well-adjusted and confident companions.

4. Education and Support

We strive to educate potential puppy owners about responsible pet ownership, breed-specific traits, and the importance of lifelong care. We provide ongoing support and guidance to our customers, ensuring they have the knowledge and resources needed to provide a loving and enriched environment for their new family member.

5. Transparency and Integrity

We maintain open and honest communication with our customers, providing them with accurate information about our breeding practices, health records, and the lineage of our puppies. We are dedicated to building trust and fostering long-term relationships based on integrity and transparency.

6. Adoption Screening and Responsible Placements

We have a thorough adoption screening process to ensure that our puppies are placed in suitable homes with responsible and committed owners. We prioritize matching the right puppy to the right family, taking into consideration the individual needs and preferences of both the puppy and the prospective owner.

7. Continuous Improvement

We are committed to continuously learning and improving our breeding practices. We stay informed about the latest advancements in veterinary care, behavior training, and responsible breeding techniques, ensuring that we provide the best possible care for our dogs and produce healthy, well-rounded puppies.

By embracing these core values, we aim to make a positive impact on the lives of our puppies, their owners, and the broader community of dog lovers.

These will be our guiding pillars that will drive everything we do, and we will never discriminate or be exclusive in any way. We check in regularly with our customers to ensure that we are living up to our mission, vision and values, and we welcome our customers to engage with us if they are not experiencing exactly what we strive to accomplish. If you ever feel that your experience does not live up to this, please contact Tom or Tara at info@summitdoodles.com and we will work diligently to make your experience a great one and to live up to our mission, vision and values.
