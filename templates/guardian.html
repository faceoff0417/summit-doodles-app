Home Guardian Program

A Guardian family raises one of our puppies as their own -- inside, loved, and part of the family -- at a significant discount, in exchange for including your dog in our breeding program.

We believe puppies and dogs should be inside, in a loving home -- not kenneled. Guardian families receive a puppy at a significantly discounted price. That discount is fully refundable once the program is complete and every term has been met.

How It Works

Female Guardians

She lives with you full time. When she's ready to be bred, she comes to us, stays through her pregnancy and the 6–7 week whelping/weaning period, then goes right back home to you. She retires from breeding between ages 4–6, is spayed, and stays with your family permanently.

Male Guardians

He lives with you full time. When he's needed for breeding, he visits us for just 7–10 days. He retires between ages 4–6, is neutered, and stays with your family permanently.

What's Covered

We Cover
  • Annual vet exams
  • Vaccines
  • Vitamins
  • Reproductive medical care
Your Family Covers
  • Approved food
  • Toys
  • Professional grooming (every 8 weeks, minimum)
  • Monthly flea/tick prevention
  • Non-breeding medical care

Requirements

Live within 60 miles of Colorado Springs, CO, with no plans to relocate for at least 5 years
Maintain regular professional grooming
Allow 24–48 hours' notice for pickups for photos, vet visits, and checkups
Notify us of any changes to your living situation, and of vacations
